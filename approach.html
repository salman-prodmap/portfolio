How I work

A clear direction.
A system for delivery.

My philosophy, principles and process for turning complex problems into products that work.

Philosophy Principles Process

01 / Philosophy

What will be the overall impact?

Before anything gets built, I ask why we need to do it and who it affects.

What looks like the biggest problem is often a symptom of a smaller one. “Our software keeps breaking” can really mean “we don’t know what’s running in our estate.” Fix that, and the bigger problem often goes away, without building anything new.

02 / Principles

Work hard to make it simple

A problem well defined is a problem half solved.

I invest most of my time defining the right problem. Get that right, and the solution is inevitable.

Simplicity takes rigorous analysis. I translate complexity into clear, testable requirements and shared understanding, enabling developers, QA teams and stakeholders to work with confidence.

03 / Process

Create systems to deliver

From the first workshop to going live in production, every step is part of a system that follows a process.

Every process is delivery-focused, with clear steps and clear outputs.

01

Audit

Discover

Structured discovery of what’s really happening.

I map the current product, user needs and competitive landscape—documenting the detail so decisions are grounded in evidence.

02

Analyse

Design

Pinpoint the problem that matters most.

I isolate the core problem: the one change that will deliver most of the value. Then I design the solution around it.

03

Advance

Deliver

Build, launch and measure.

I execute. No fluff. Just forward motion.

Then back to Audit: measure what shipped, and go again.
