From concept to global platform

Strategic Initiative

Reward, originally a bespoke solution provider, carved a niche in the UK market, serving major financial institutions with tailor-made technology solutions for loyalty programs. With a legacy of delivering £1 billion in cashback over the last decade, Reward set its sights on global expansion, spurred by the directive to find a solution. As Senior Product Manager and Architecture Lead, I took on the challenge and transformed Reward into a major global player in the fintech industry.

The loyalty platform

Connecting retailers, banks and consumers.

Transformation

The transformation journey encompassed three pivotal challenges:

  1. Strategic Alliance: I spearheaded efforts to forge strategic partnerships vital for global expansion. This involved establishing alliances with industry giants like VISA, facilitating Reward’s entry into new markets and unlocking growth opportunities.
  2. Platform Design: I led the conceptualization and development of the Global Merchant Marketplace (GMM) platform, a sophisticated API-based loyalty solution. My strategic vision and meticulous approach ensured the platform’s scalability, adaptability, and seamless integration across diverse banking ecosystems.
  3. Global Service: Anticipating the challenge of servicing a global customer base, I devised and implemented a comprehensive servicing target operating model (TOM). Underpinned by my brainchild, the “Service Hive,” this innovative solution streamlined customer query management, ensuring efficient and effective service delivery worldwide. My innovation extended to API-based servicing, incorporating language considerations and reducing query volumes by 80% through smart resolutions, providing customers with reasons for cashback discrepancies.

Rapid Deployment

My leadership was instrumental in reshaping Reward’s trajectory. I championed the GMM platform, a revolutionary API-based loyalty solution that facilitated seamless engagement between retailers, banks, and consumers. Collaborating with cross-functional teams, I navigated the complexities of product management and architecture, ensuring alignment with organizational goals. My efforts transformed Reward into a major global player and paved the way for strategic partnerships, including one with VISA.

Outstanding Results

Under my guidance, the GMM platform was successfully deployed at major banks worldwide, including Warba Bank in Kuwait, First Abu Dhabi Bank in the UAE, and Monzo Bank in the UK. My meticulous approach and strategic vision accelerated the onboarding process, driving rapid market penetration and revenue growth. The partnership with VISA further solidified Reward’s position as a key player in the fintech ecosystem, opening new avenues for collaboration and innovation.

The implementation of the GMM platform yielded impressive results for Reward’s global expansion efforts:

  • 30% Increase in Customer Engagement: Achieved a 30% increase in customer engagement through targeted retail offers.
  • 15 Million+ Global Customers: Successfully served over 15 million customers worldwide.
  • 80% Reduction in Servicing Queries: Implemented automated solutions resulting in an 80% reduction in servicing queries, enhancing operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.
  • Real-Time Cashback Processing: Gained a competitive edge through real-time cashback processing, leading to wins over competitors and securing partnerships with major players like Monzo Bank in the UK.

My leadership and collaborative efforts were instrumental in achieving these remarkable outcomes.

Conclusion

My journey from a directive to find a solution to spearheading the discovery, development, and global scaling of the GMM platform exemplifies the transformative power of effective leadership and collaboration. Through my efforts, Reward transitioned from a bespoke solution provider to a major global player, forming strategic partnerships and driving significant growth. My role serves as a testament to the impact of visionary leadership and proactive initiative in reshaping industries and driving innovation on a global scale.
